A 100-word villain monologue for a Dark Lord who is secretly just very tired — no battle, just admin: a broken throne, a Facilities ticket open since the last age, conquest postponed by paperwork, and a scheme that amounts to monologuing until everyone can go home early.

> You stand before me, hero, sword drawn, prophecy under your arm like a lunchbox. Good. Sit down. No, not the throne. The stool. A spring pokes through the throne, and Facilities has sat on my ticket since the last age. I meant to conquer the north this spring, but the paperwork alone would take three winters, and my generals schedule wars around their holidays. So here is my scheme, such as it is: I will monologue. You will listen. We will both pretend this is destiny. If nobody objects, we all go home early. The darkness can wait until Monday.

## Why this monologue

- **The tiredness is felt, never announced:** the Dark Lord never says he is tired — it leaks out of the voice instead. He greets the hero like a receptionist, offers the stool because the throne is broken, and treats the apocalypse as a scheduling problem.
- **Still a villain, with a scheme and a history:** he has a ticket open since the last age, a conquest planned for spring, generals who book wars around their holidays. The menace is all there; it has just been routed through Facilities.
- **Every word earns its place:** exactly 100 words, each one doing work — the lunchbox prophecy, the spring in the throne, the three winters of paperwork — and the punchline, "The darkness can wait until Monday," lands because the whole speech has been stalling for it.
- **Built to play in a game:** short beats, a voice an actor can chew on, and a joke the player gets in the first line and keeps getting.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the monologue (exactly 100 words, 550 characters, no trailing newline) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`), after a self-check mirroring the sealed gate: UTF-8, 1–600 characters, exactly 100 words, not only whitespace, no leading/trailing whitespace, no control or invisible format characters. Pure stdlib only.
